Questions and answers

Short answers to what parents ask most.

Getting started

What is KINND?

A private place where parents and their family share everything about their  children.

Is it an app?

It runs in your browser and works like an app. Add it to your home screen. No app store needed.

Which languages?

English, Danish and Swedish.

Family and access

Does the other parent need to pay?

No. Your co-parent is included in your plan when you invite them.

What can grandparents see?

Only what you share with them. Each adult sees what their role allows.

Who can invite family?

Parents. Grandparents and caregivers need the Family plan.

Paying

What does it cost?

Free is 0 DKK. Parents is 29 DKK a month. Family is 69 DKK a month.

What happens after the 30 free days?

You pick a plan. If you pick nothing, you move to Free.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. You keep your plan until the period ends.

Safety

Is our data safe?

Media and sensitive information are encrypted. Sign-in requires two steps.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Your data = your choice. Download or delete your account on demand.

Does KINND help with conflicts?

No. KINND is a place to share in. We do not take sides.
