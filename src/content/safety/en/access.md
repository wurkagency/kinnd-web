---
title: "Who can see what"
---

## 1. Families are kept apart

Every family's data is kept apart from every other family's. The database itself enforces it, not just the app on top of it.

## 2. Roles

Each adult sees only what their relationship to the child allows.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Parent or guardian | Manages the child, invites others and decides what they see |
| Family | Can post moments, add to lists and request appointments |
| Caregiver | Can view, comment and claim list items |

Parents can remove a family member at any time. Access is checked on every request, so a removed person loses access at once.

Medical information is shown to family only when a parent allows it.

## 3. Signing in

- Two-step sign-in is always on, for everyone. The second step is a one-time code (TOTP).
- Your phone number and email address are verified.
- You can also sign in with Google or Microsoft.
- You can see the devices you are signed in on, and sign out any of them.

## 4. What our team can see

**In normal use, nobody at KINND sees your photos, messages or your child's information.** Our management system works on technical signals only.

**When an alarm goes off,** our team first sees masked technical details of that case: sign-ins, devices, IP addresses and the warnings that fired. Names and personal details stay masked.

**If there are grounds to look further,** we follow the protocols of the Danish police's National Cyber Crime Centre (NC3). A second senior reviewer checks the case before it is escalated.

## 5. Every look is logged

Every time an employee views details in a case, including masked views, it is logged and audited.

The audit log is part of every report we hand to the police, so the authorities can see who viewed what, and when.

## 6. How our own systems are protected

- Cases are handled in a separate management system with its own sign-in. It runs apart from the app.
- Staff sign in with company accounts and strong multi-factor authentication. There is no public login page.
- Each staff role sees only what its job needs.
- Staff can only open a family's data inside a case, with a stated reason.
- Medical information never reaches the staff tools.

## 7. How long technical data is kept

IP addresses, device IDs and photo metadata used in investigations are kept for up to one year. Read the [privacy notice](../../legal/privacy/) for every retention period.
