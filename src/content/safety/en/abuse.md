## 1. Abuse Central

Abuse Central is our warning engine. It runs around the clock and uses a wide set of triggers and patterns to spot signs of misuse, such as:

- unusual sign-ins
- accounts created to reach a child
- attempts to get access someone should not have

It works on technical signals only. It does not read your photos or messages.

## 2. People decide

Abuse Central only raises alarms. Every alarm is checked by a person on our team, and serious actions are always decided by a named person.

We do not use automated decisions with legal effect.

## 3. How a case is handled

1. An alarm or a report opens a case.
2. Our team reviews the masked technical details: sign-ins, devices, IP addresses and the warnings that fired.
3. If there are grounds to look further, we follow the protocols of the Danish police's National Cyber Crime Centre (NC3), and a second senior reviewer checks the case before it is escalated.
4. If the terms or the law have been broken, we can remove content, restrict features, or suspend or close an account. You get the reason and can complain.
5. Confirmed illegal cases are handed to the police.

Every look at a case is logged. See [Who can see what](../access/).

## 4. Reporting a problem

Worried about an account or a child's space? Write to <abuse@kinnd.eu>. Every report gets a case that a person follows to the end.

This address is also our point of contact for authorities and users under the EU Digital Services Act.

In the app, parents can remove a family member from their child at any time.

## 5. Working with the police

When we confirm an illegal case, or suspect a serious crime, we hand it to the police with the information they need. That includes the audit log of who at KINND viewed what.

We follow each case until it is closed.

## 6. We stay out of family conflicts

KINND is a private space to share in. We do not take sides or settle disagreements, and we never remove a parent because the other parent asked.
