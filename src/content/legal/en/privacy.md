This policy explains how WURK ApS processes personal data when you visit kinnd.eu or use the KINND app at app.kinnd.eu. KINND is a private space where parents and family coordinate daily life around their children and share information, messages and media.

This is a translation. If the Danish and English versions differ, the Danish version applies.

## 1. Data controller

WURK ApS, CVR no. 37547395, Skoleholdervej 91, 2400 Copenhagen NV, Denmark, is the data controller.

Send questions about personal data to <privacy@kinnd.eu>.

## 2. The data we process

| Category | Data | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | Name, email, phone number, password (encrypted), two-step login details, language and country | You |
| Family and relations | Who you are connected to as parent, co-parent, guardian or family member, and invitations | You and those who invite you |
| Child profiles | The child's name, date of birth, profile picture and what you choose to add, such as appointments, custody schedule, notes, school or daycare and contacts | Parents and guardians |
| Content | Calendar events, messages, notes, photos, videos and other files | You and those you share with |
| Media metadata | Original files are stored with their embedded metadata (EXIF), such as time, device and any GPS position | The files you upload |
| Subscription and payment | Plan, trial period, payment status, invoices, country and VAT. Card details are handled by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers | You and the payment provider |
| Technical and security | IP address, device and browser details, device ID, login history and log files | Your device |
| Communication | Support requests and the emails and text messages we send you | You and us |

**Sensitive data.** KINND does not require sensitive data. If you choose to add, for example, health information about yourself or a child, we process it only to show it to the people you share with.

**Data about children and others.** Only adults aged 18 or over can create an account. Children do not have their own login. When you add data about a child or another person, you are responsible for having the right to do so, for example as holder of parental responsibility or with that person's acceptance.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Create and run your account and provide the service, including sharing with the people you have chosen | Performance of our contract with you, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Process data about children and other persons that users add | Our and the users' legitimate interest in coordinating the children's daily life, Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Process sensitive data you choose to add | Explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a). For children, consent is given by the holder of parental responsibility |
| Send service messages by email and text, such as login codes and notices about subscription and deletion | The contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Payment, invoicing and bookkeeping | The contract and legal obligation under the Danish Bookkeeping Act, Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) |
| Security, mandatory two-step login, and preventing fraud and unauthorised access to children's data | Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Investigate reports of illegal content and misuse and pass confirmed cases to authorities | Legitimate interest and legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c) and (f), Art. 9(2)(f), and section 8 of the Danish Data Protection Act |
| Newsletters and marketing | Your consent, Art. 6(1)(a), and section 10 of the Danish Marketing Practices Act |
| Statistics and non-essential cookies | Your consent under the Danish Cookie Order |

You can withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

We do not use your data for automated decisions or profiling with legal effect, and we do not sell personal data.

## 4. Media and location data

We store original photos and videos with their metadata so you can download them again in full quality. GPS position in metadata is not shown to other users and is removed from the copy you download. We use it only where necessary to investigate a specific case of misuse or unlawful access.

## 5. Recipients

We share data only when necessary:

- **Other users:** the parents, guardians and family members you are connected to see the content shared with them.
- **Processors:** suppliers that process data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
- **Authorities:** the police and other authorities when the law requires it, or when we have confirmed an unlawful matter. In those cases we may disclose user data, device IDs, IP addresses and relevant content.
- **Advisers and business transfers:** auditor, lawyer and any buyer of the service, under confidentiality.

| Processor | Task | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brevo (Sendinblue SAS) | Sending email and text messages | EU (France) |
| Quickpay ApS | Payment gateway | EU (Denmark) |
| Hetzner GmbH | Operations, database and file storage | EU (Germany) |

## 6. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

We store the data in the EU/EEA. If a supplier exceptionally processes data outside the EU/EEA, this happens only on the basis of an adequacy decision from the European Commission or the Commission's standard contractual clauses. Contact us for a copy of the basis.

## 7. How long we keep data

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Account, child profiles and content | As long as the account is active |
| Account after an expired subscription where usage exceeds the Free plan | Deleted 90 days after the subscription expires. You are notified 90, 30 and 15 days before, with the exact date |
| Account you delete yourself | Deleted without undue delay and no later than 30 days after. Backups are overwritten within 7 days |
| Bookkeeping records, such as invoices | 5 years from the end of the financial year |
| Security logs | 12 months |
| Cases of misuse or unlawful matters | As long as the case is pending and claims can be made |
| Support requests | 1 years after the case is closed |

If you are the only parent on a child profile and delete your account, we also delete the child's data. You confirm the deletion through an emailed link that expires after 24 hours.

## 8. Security

We protect the data with, among other things, encrypted connections, mandatory two-step login, access control and logging. If a security breach creates a high risk for you, we tell you without undue delay.

## 9. Cookies

kinnd.eu and the app use necessary cookies and local storage for login, security and your settings. Cookies for statistics or marketing are set only if you have consented in the cookie banner, where you can also change your choice.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access the data we process about you
- have inaccurate data corrected
- have data erased
- have processing restricted
- object to processing based on legitimate interest
- receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format (data portability)
- withdraw consent

Write to <privacy@kinnd.eu> to use your rights. We reply within one month. Holders of parental responsibility can exercise the rights on the child's behalf.

## 11. Complaints

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark, [datatilsynet.dk](https://www.datatilsynet.dk), or to the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live. We would like to hear from you first so we can try to resolve the matter.

## 12. Changes

We update this policy when the service or the rules change. For material changes, we notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
