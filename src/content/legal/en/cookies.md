This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies WURK ApS uses on kinnd.eu and in the KINND app at app.kinnd.eu, and why. It adds to our [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/).

This is a translation. If the Danish and English versions differ, the Danish version applies.

## 1. The short version

- We only use cookies and local storage that the website and the app need to work.
- We do not use cookies for statistics, advertising or tracking.
- We do not use third-party cookies. No one else can follow you through KINND.
- Because of this, we do not show a cookie banner.

## 2. What cookies and local storage are

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves in your browser and reads again on your next visit. Local storage is similar, but it stays in your browser and is not sent to us with every request. The Danish Cookie Order covers both, and so does this policy.

## 3. On kinnd.eu

kinnd.eu sets no cookies. Fonts and images come from our own server, so visiting the website sends nothing to third parties.

| Name | Type | Purpose | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| kinnd-lang | Local storage | Remembers the language you chose, so the website opens in it next time | Until you clear it in your browser |

## 4. In the app

The app at app.kinnd.eu uses one cookie and saves a few settings on your device.

| Name | Type | Purpose | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| kinnd.sid | Cookie | Keeps you signed in and protects your session. It holds only a random session ID, is sent only over an encrypted connection, and cannot be read by scripts on the page. | 90 days after you last used the app. It is removed when you sign out. |
| App settings | Local storage | Remembers choices on this device: your theme, which children you are viewing, recent searches and tips you have closed | Until you clear it in your browser |
| App files | Cache storage | Keeps a copy of the app's own files, so it starts quickly and can be added to your home screen. It holds no personal data. | Until a new version replaces it or you clear it |

## 5. Why we do not ask for consent

The Danish Cookie Order does not require consent for cookies and local storage that are strictly necessary to provide a service you have asked for. Everything above is in that group.

If we ever want to use cookies for statistics or marketing, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

## 6. How to remove them

You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. If you delete the app's cookie, you are signed out. If you delete kinnd-lang, the website picks a language for you again.
